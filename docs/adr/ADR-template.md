## Review rubric (mentor grades your ADR on these)

1. **Three genuine options** — not one real option and two straw men.
2. **Evidence, not vibes** — a measured number per option, from the same dev set.
3. **Named constraint** — the decision is anchored to something from the charter.
4. **Honest weaknesses** — the chosen option's weaknesses stated as clearly as the rejected ones'.
5. **Falsifiable revisit trigger** — a condition someone else could check.
6. **Forecloses stated** — you know what you gave up.

Fewer than five of six: it comes back with the specific gap named.
